The sensor readings table is the most detailed one. Each row is one minute of a trip. From there you can trace back to which trip it belongs to, and then to the driver and vehicle involved in that trip.

Drivers Table - 30 rows

Driver_ID
Unique code for each driver (D01 to D30)
Driver_Name
Driver's full name
Age
Age in years
Gender
Male or Female
License_Experience_Years
How many years they have held a license
Date_Joined_Fleet
When they joined VexarDrive
Primary_Vehicle_ID
The vehicle normally assigned to them
Home_Hub
The area they normally operate from

Vehicles Table - 30 rows

Vehicle_ID
Unique code for each vehicle (V01 to V30)
Vehicle_Type
All are two-wheelers (scooter or motorcycle)
Make and Model
Brand and model name
Manufacture_Year
Year the vehicle was made (2019 to 2025)
Registration_Date
When it was registered
Odometer_KM_Start_of_Week
Total km on the clock at the start of the week
Last_Service_Date
Date of the most recent workshop visit

Trips Table - 450 rows

Trip_ID
Unique code for each trip (T00001 to T00450)
Driver_ID and Vehicle_ID
Links to the driver and vehicle for this trip
Trip_Date
The date the trip happened
Start_Time and End_Time
When the trip started and ended
Duration_Min
How long the trip lasted in minutes
Distance_KM
Distance covered, worked out from per-minute speed
Avg_Speed_kmph
Average speed over the whole trip
Max_Speed_kmph
Fastest speed recorded during the trip
Start and End Coordinates
GPS location where the trip began and ended

Sensor Readings Table - 12,987 rows

One row per minute per trip. This is where all the sensor data lives and where the scores come from.

Trip_ID, Driver_ID, Vehicle_ID
Links back to the other three tables
Timestamp
Exact time of this sensor reading
Latitude and Longitude
GPS position at this minute
Speed_kmph
How fast the vehicle was going
Accel_X_g
Forward/backward force in g. Negative means braking, positive means accelerating.
Accel_Y_g
Left/right force in g
Accel_Z_g
Up/down force in g. Should be around 1.0g on flat road (that is just gravity).
Gyro_X_dps
Rolling speed in degrees per second
Gyro_Y_dps
Pitching speed in degrees per second
Gyro_Z_dps
Turning speed in degrees per second. Used to detect sharp corners.
